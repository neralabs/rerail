The dashboard follows the v9 design. Colours are Nera's tokens: titles in Nera's dark indigo warmed toward brand (foreground in dark), Steer in warning text on a faint warning tint and Log in muted grey, danger in Nera's danger text and tint, and a page background that glows from the bottom-right corner. Every column of violation counts is headed "Violations", aligned with its numbers, and Recent sessions shows only that column. A pack page leads with its name and an outline Remove pack, then what the pack covers, then its counts, where Remove's question opens; its whole-pack switch, labelled All, is on only when every rule is. The sidebar shows "20/21" beside a pack and wraps a long name. The Rerail wordmark opens Overview. The Violations steered tile opens History by rule with its new Steered chip (All · Steered · Logged, kept in the address as `#history?outcome=`), and Sessions with violations opens History by session filtered to them (`#sessions?filter=violations`). A session's header counts its violations; its timeline filters by one rule from a dropdown, with or without Show all events, and the event-type chips and Copy id are gone. The rule sheet says Block is not in this release only on the Block button and its toast. A pack removed from the catalog asks on a line of its own and reads Removed with Undo until the page is left. On a wide window an open sheet widens the page by its own width instead of narrowing the content.
