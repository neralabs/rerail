Rule packs published by third parties load: an npm package named `rerail-pack-<id>` or `@<scope>/rerail-pack-<id>`, added with `rerail packs add <package>`. Rerail names its publisher, warns that it has not reviewed it, and loads its own pack when ids clash. A pack added from a folder or tarball counts as not reviewed too ([third parties' packs](docs/rule-packs.md#third-parties-packs)).
