6. The decisions

Compile before the cases, or give the one case a longer limit?

We chose the compile before the cases: each case times its own commands, and no limit grows with the compile.

Would change our mind: CI runners so slow that the case's own commands outlast its 5 s limit. Splitting the case would then win.
