6. The decisions

Stop the pump at an edit queued ahead, or bound the old hold by time?

We chose the pump stop: the case sets up the order it checks, and no wait rests on timing.

Would change our mind: A maintainer who wants this case to keep the other order too, where the pump takes the older edit before the newer one arrives.
