The pack is no longer limited to Rerail 0.1.x, so updating Rerail past 0.1.x no longer leaves it out. This version needs the Rerail released with it, or a newer one: update Rerail when you update the pack (`rerail update`). An older Rerail leaves the pack out and says so.
