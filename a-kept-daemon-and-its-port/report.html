5. The changes

Each line opens its card.

ASessions keep autostart offMedium riskUnder the setting, an OMP session's start started a daemon in each of 5 runs; here in none.
Problem
A person who runs the daemon themselves gets one started, or theirs ended, by an OMP or Pi session, and /rerail-start shows a link that refuses the connection.
Who meets it
Anyone with RERAIL_AUTOSTART=0 in OMP, or in Pi from a managed install; the defects round showed each case 5 of 5 on Linux x64.
What it does
attachedDaemon looks the daemon up under the setting through usableDaemon, the check a hook's session start makes; AttachRetry.attach shows the hooks' line (autostartOffLine) and keeps trying; rerail start prints that line, marked △, in place of the link and its reason.
Risk
Medium: every OMP and Pi attach goes through attachedDaemon; with autostart on it calls startDaemonForPackage as before.
Check
That attachedDaemon starts nothing under the setting, that usableDaemon is the one rule both callers apply, and that AttachRetry.attach resolves only for the two autostart errors and rethrows every other failure.
BA replacement keeps the portLow riskAfter a hook's replacement, the link rerail start showed answered in none of 5 runs; here in all.
Problem
When a hook replaces another package's daemon that took a free port, the new one moves, and the link rerail start just showed refuses the connection.
Who meets it
Homes shared by two Rerail packages, as after an update, or OMP or Pi of one install beside Claude Code, Codex or Cursor of another; 5 of 5 on Linux x64.
What it does
--prefer-port: a daemon started in place of another tries that daemon's port first, then the remembered port, 4737 and a free one as before, and never remembers the port it tried first (remembersPort). keptOrReplaced, replacedOlderDaemon and relaunchArguments pass it.
Risk
Low: one more listen before the ports tried today; an explicit port still wins, and a port another program took costs no failed start.
Check
listenPreferred and candidatePorts: the preferred port first, the order after it as before; remembersPort: the port tried first is never written, a port given with --port always is.

6. The decisions

How should a session's start answer when the setting keeps it from a daemon?

We chose show the hooks' line and keep trying quietly, so the session attaches once the daemon runs.

Would change our mind: A maintainer who wants such a session to stop trying until the person restarts it.

Options and evidence
OptionWhat a user getsCostWhat it breaks
Show the hooks' line; keep trying in the background chosenThe person learns why nothing is recorded; the session attaches once they start the daemon.One lookup per try, at most every 30 s.Nothing found.
Fail the session's start, as any attach failure doesNo new path.The person's own choice shows as an error.The hooks' rule: the setting is not a failure.
Show the line; stop tryingNo tries.The session never attaches until it restarts.What a hook's next event does: it finds the daemon once it runs.

Evidence. The hooks' answer (answerAutostartOff) and AttachRetry since #1161; the integration cases show the line once per start, and no daemon after it.

Which port should a daemon started in place of another take?

We chose the old daemon's port first, then as before, as our comment on #1192 said.

Would change our mind: A maintainer who wants a replacement to bind the old port or fail.

Options and evidence

Evidence. The defects round's probe: 0 of 5 links answered before, 5 of 5 here; the daemon's start cases on both systems, the preferred port held and free.

Limits that stay.

9. What we ask of you

Asks 1 and 2 rule on answers 1 and 2.

  1. Under RERAIL_AUTOSTART=0, may OMP and Pi sessions start and replace no daemon, as hooks do? We recommend yes: the docs give the setting one meaning.
  2. May a daemon started in place of another try the old daemon's port first? We recommend yes: the link shown before keeps answering.
